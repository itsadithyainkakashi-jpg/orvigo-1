# Restore the old Fashion collections

## What will change

- Reconnect the Fashion page to the existing saved Fashion product records instead of showing the unrelated local catalog first.
- Arrange the old Men collections in their established order: New Arrivals, Casual Shirts, Striped Shirts, T-Shirts, Sweatshirts, and Casual Pants & Trousers.
- Keep each saved product attached to its original name, price, category, collection, and uploaded image.
- Preserve the existing product-card appearance, product details, wishlist, and Add to Cart behavior.
- Keep the saved records untouched; no deletion, reset, replacement imagery, or generated imagery.

## Reliability

- Show collection banners and local uploaded assets while saved records load.
- Handle missing or empty collections without hiding the collections that still contain products.
- Keep refresh behavior stable by loading from the saved records each time.

## Scope

- Change only the Fashion page and its Fashion data presentation.
- Do not modify Grocery, Medicine, Home, Profile, Cart, or other sections.

## Verification

- Open Fashion and confirm the old collection order appears.
- Open New Arrivals and confirm all nine saved products and their original images load.
- Open a product, use Wishlist and Add to Cart, then refresh and confirm the collection remains available.
